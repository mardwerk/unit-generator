The inspection found recognizable source connections and preserved hard constraints. It also found prose that overstates mechanical choices: for example, a description may suggest broad group control while the structured delivery target count remains narrow. That is a limitation of design quality and prose checking, even when static checks pass. Numeric values and gameplay balance have not been tested in a game runtime.

Several full classic drafts exceeded an initial 120-second smoke-test limit. A transport diagnostic completed a full draft in about 125 seconds. The smoke check now permits 300 seconds per call and 900 seconds total, retaining one repair. Some configured-proxy calls failed intermittently; those failures returned research rather than accepted content. This sample is too small to estimate reliability or claim an improvement over direct prompting.

The controlled comparison from the plan remains a separate human evaluation: six varied requests, three repeats per direct/single-draft/concept-selection strategy, equal source/rule/example exposure and repair budgets, blinded preferences, and separate validity, constraint, latency and usage reports. The early smoke check does not replace it. The single-draft strategy remains the default; no quality gain from extra concept calls is claimed.

Run `node scripts/live-smoke.mjs --discovery --out <new-file>` after building and configuring a model. `--case 'Monkey D. Luffy'` selects one fixed case. Exports contain complete results; no reports are written implicitly. CI uses fake transports.
