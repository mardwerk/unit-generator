## What the alternatives establish

PR5's `src/core/universal.ts` has a useful checked-candidate boundary and a model stub that throws if offline generation accidentally calls a provider. Its `examples/showcase` artifacts are potential negative quality controls precisely because a structural pass is insufficient. Every Goku path cites a fact about childhood physical blows despite energy branches; every Zoro path cites rare hand-to-hand combat despite sword/projectile branches. `joinEvidence` assigns fact zero to every path. `deriveTraits` does not represent ownership, negation or continuity. Its fitness function rewards one highest-scoring build, not three valuable branches or an evidenced repertoire. A bounded offline comparator can expose these differences without promoting its 823-line module or replacing the purchase plan.

PR6's `src/core/spines.ts` selects fixed progressions with ordered regular expressions. In `examples/spine-gallery.html`, "Goku (dragon ball)" receives an elemental burn kit resembling Natsu's because the dragon match precedes the Goku match. `src/core/compact.ts` lets the model write mechanics and prices despite instructions to copy spine prices, and assigns evidence by position rather than relevance. It omits prior-result and feedback interpretation from the compact prompt. These are source and revision limitations, not defects a legal-build count can resolve.

The transferable PR6 observation is narrower: its report describes tiny base follow-ups surviving validation and repair, then a successful regeneration after those fields were removed from the provider grammar. That is reported historical evidence, not a reproduced causal benchmark. Main can prevent shapes already forbidden by the supplied Definition while retaining local validation and explicit custom-Definition support. Base follow-up and distribution restrictions must follow the actual policy, not become universal mechanics prohibitions. This adaptation preserves repertoire and purchase commitments. Its implementation and offline checks do not by themselves prove improved generation quality.

PR7's `research/btd6/PROGRAM.md` usefully asks for one hypothesis, fixed checks and inspection of a completed batch before the next change. Its 30-generation proposal is not a spending authorization: with two stages and one correction per stage, 30 samples can require 120 authoring calls before optional reviews. The reproduced main dry run planned five samples, at most 20 authoring calls, no reviews and no dispatch. Use the existing evaluator's stops and artifacts. Record the failure, affected purchase, single change, pinned runtime and sources, frozen development and holdout cases, authorized remaining budget including reservations, maximum calls, outcomes, human findings and keep/drop decision. A reused development case is not an untouched holdout.

PR8's `scripts/score-blueprint-judge.mjs` reproduced five reference scores of 100, duplicate-T1 and second-manual mutations at 49 with policy veto, and a numeric-only T3 mutation at 80 without veto. The last result does not independently prove a bad purchase: the default allows focused numeric specialization. The score counts source indices without reading their support, exact attack differences without measuring distinct purchasing situations, and any doubled specialty metric without establishing capstone value. Its veto reuses `designPolicyIssues`. It does not replace the complete evidence and 64-build checks, and tying every nominally good recipe does not demonstrate useful ranking.

PR8's role probe reproduced 58/390, or 14.9%, on leave-one-tower-out prediction. Its printed 76/390 majority baseline, or 19.5%, chooses the majority using all labels, including the held-out tower. Recomputing majority from each training fold gives 29/390, or 7.4%; the classifier beats that fitted comparator. Basic DPS and splash have 76 and 72 records, so withholding a tower can swap the majority. These are 390 correlated upgrades in 26 tower groups, not 390 independent quality judgments. One crude word-vote classifier neither demonstrates useful semantic learning nor establishes that learning cannot work. Further research would need training-only baselines, per-class results, tower-grouped uncertainty, negative controls and an actual target aligned with generated-unit quality.

The BTD6 package has zero populated post-upgrade combat-stat records. Its categories are editorial; its patch and repository revisions are unpinned. See the [dataset notes](../research/btd6/raw/btd6_README.txt). Fixed prices cover 78 upgrades per tier except T5, which has 77 because Monkeyopolis is conditional. The scorer's printed T5/T4 ratio denominator of 78 is wrong; independently computed valid ratios number 77, with median 6.25. Price overlap between T4 and T5 refutes a universal tier threshold, not every conditional use of prices. No outcome or acceptance labels in this package validate a quality scorer.

## Design guidance and the next comparison

The anime-to-BTD6 pitch is useful where it asks what the player buys, how behavior develops and why an alternative matters. Keep varied progressions, six scoped complete crosspaths, bounded control and support, and explicit weaknesses. A named form should implement a supported purchase or remain a clearly stated omission; repeating forms across tiers is not a substitute for progression. Source ownership, continuity, canon limitations and adaptation choices remain separate from mechanical legality.

Universal DPS price bands, automatic whole-kit archetype mapping and a single power score are not justified by the retained evidence. Incremental upgrade prices differ from complete-build investment. Damage divided by interval is a conditional attack-component estimate; splash, pierce, targeting, uptime, immunities, support and economy prevent it from being a universal value measure. Compare capstones with affordable T4 copies under stated space and target-access assumptions, and compare legal secondary purchases in their actual builds. Label proposed bands as design assumptions until controlled gameplay observations support them.

The executable Definition owns immunity behavior. Its starter `normal: []` damage-immunity list does not establish a universal BTD6 fact or remove separate targeting and control restrictions. Neither anime naming nor a reference example grants unsupported bypasses, recursive chaining, durability or runtime systems.

The smallest adoption is Definition-aware grammar narrowing, a few adversarial purchase/evidence cases, and a trial record attached to the existing evaluation. PR5's legal but weak examples can supply negative controls; PR8's mutations should include legitimate numeric progression as a control against overstrict rules. Record existing human accept/reject decisions with reasons and candidate versions, without pretending collection adds no expert effort. Keep source evidence, canon review and all 64 legal-build checks intact.
