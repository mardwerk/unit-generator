Can a generated result still be checked after editing?

Throwaway logic prototype. In-memory only. Open this HTML file directly.

A valid generation returned 4,240,558 bytes. Checking that result sent 4,250,118 bytes to a route limited to 4 MiB and failed. This demo compares that route limit with a separate 40 MiB edit limit. It also shows why an edit made during a check must remain unvalidated when the earlier check finishes.

The generation limit remains 4 MiB. The prototype keeps all source evidence; transport limits do not change whether a unit passes its rules.
