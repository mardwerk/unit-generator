Checked September 22, 2026 for the [reshape proposal](../docs/GENERATOR-RESHAPE.md). These are technical documentation and reference entry points, not additional empirical evidence that the proposed generator produces good designs. Existing research is summarized in [generation approaches](GENERATION-APPROACHES.md). The workspace root `GAME-DESIGN-RESEARCH.md` contains the wider 38-paper catalogue; it is optional and is not required to use this repository.

The research connections are GAVEL for executable variation, the RTS unit-generation paper for explicit ability configuration and evaluator limits, Mortar for assessing mechanics in games, Mechanic Miner for generating a supporting challenge, and GameASG-Bench for separating checks from whole-task correctness. These motivate separate interpretation, mechanics and evaluation artifacts. None demonstrates that a ruleset schema will select the right character organization.

Primary links: [GAVEL](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.09388v2), [RTS units](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.03387v1), [Mortar](https://www.raillab.org/publication/nasir-2026-mortar/nasir-2026-mortar.pdf), [Mechanic Miner](https://www.possibilityspace.org/papers/evo13.pdf), [GameASG-Bench](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.21293v1), [quality diversity survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.04053), [LLM ideation and self-evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.04109). Detailed findings and limitations remain in the workspace catalogue and earlier reviews; no new replication was performed.

The conversation's YAML packs, interface names, candidate budgets, price multipliers, Luffy upgrades and form formula are proposals, not published research results or implemented APIs. The selected architecture should preserve the repository's existing Definition/Profile terminology, source ownership and Engine boundaries. The public FOSS distribution and its reference content require an explicit release scope; this source review does not establish a license or exact BTD6 compatibility.
