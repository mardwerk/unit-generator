Summary

Wizard Monkeys are capable of learning a variety of different spells dedicated for damage and utility. Generally, their spells are quite accurate but have a noteworthy weakness to Purple Bloons without Necromancer: Unpopped Army. The lower-tier upgrades generally are fairly weak alone, with exception of Wall of Fire.

Tips
0-0-0 Wizard is cheaper than a 1-0-0 Dart.
