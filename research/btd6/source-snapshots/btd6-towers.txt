“	Tower defense is about what towers you use and where you put them.	”
	~ BTD1 Round 13 Pre-Round Comment	

All four BTD6 tower classes represented by towers on Encrypted: Primary (Dart Monkey), Military (Sniper Monkey), Magic (Wizard Monkey), and Support (Engineer Monkey).

A Tower (or Monkey in BTD6 and BTDB2) is a unit that helps the player fight off (or at least contribute to forces against) Bloons. Towers are the essential elements of the Bloons TD series, encompassing the entirety of a player's defenses, each tower contributing in the battle to beat the Bloons throughout the games. Most types of towers are infantry units that cost in-game cash. Unless specified by special challenge rules, there is no limit to how many of these infantry units can be placed, although most towers each occupy a footprint that consumes space. These units are often referred as just "towers". However, there are special types of tower that have different requirements for unlocking and placement, such as Special Agents and placeable Powers (which cost Monkey Money), as well as Heroes (which automatically level up and become stronger every round) and Sub-Towers (descendants of parent towers).

Throughout the Bloons TD series, every tower except the Banana Farm can attack bloons without upgrades (Monkey Village can attack with the High Energy Beacon and Primary Expertise upgrades). All towers are unique, with increasing power generally accompanied by increasing cost. Each tower contains upgrades that boost the power of itself and/or other towers.

Many of the towers are either monkeys, units maintained by a monkey, or units owned by a monkey, with the exception of Tack Shooter, Bomb Tower, Ice Tower (between BTD1 to BTD4), and Banana Farm (unless upgraded to Monkey Bank or above, or Marketplace or above).

In the first Bloons TD game, there were 5 towers: the Dart Monkey, Tack Shooter, Super Monkey, Ice Tower and Bomb Tower. Each game adds more towers than the previous one.

Starting from Bloons TD 5, Sub-Towers have been introduced, originating from a particular tower type with special stats that can be inherited from their parent. Special variants of towers called Heroes have been added in BTD6 and BTDB2. Heroes function much differently from ordinary towers, although they can be both considered towers and not considered towers depending on how they are viewed.

Trivia
Most of the towers are considered genderless in-game. These are confirmed by many of the in-game descriptions of each tower and their upgrades, alongside Ninja Kiwi often referring each of them by genderless "it".
According to Ninja Kiwi's 3rd June 2022 blog, Ninja Kiwi states that there are monkeys in everything, no matter how small. This also implies all non-monkey towers too.
On the same blog, Ninja Kiwi said that monkeys and non-monkey towers cannot move without special mobility such as Support Chinook, as each those towers are content with their own spots.
According to Ninja Kiwi's 28th April 2023 blog, all Monkeys are given a choice to specialize as a specific tower type.
