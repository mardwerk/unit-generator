Strategy
See also: Monkey Village (BTD6)/Strategies
Summary

Monkey Villages have almost no offensive capabilities (except Primary Expertise's mega ballista attack), instead providing nearby towers with ranged buffs that empower affected towers. Since most of its buffs are local, it is best to compress as many towers within a Monkey Village's influence range as possible to obtain the optimal benefits from the Monkey Village. The Village can also synergize with other Villages, such as buffing influence ranges of each other. Multiple buffs can be applied to most of the affected towers, including some exclusive buffs that only affect Primary Monkeys (such as Primary Training).

Tips
Multiple nearby Monkey Commerce Villages can be utilized for a bigger discount to base towers and upgrades up to Tier 3. These can self-synergize with other Villages, making their upgrades cheaper and increasing range.
