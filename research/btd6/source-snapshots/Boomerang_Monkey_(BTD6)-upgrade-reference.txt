The Boomerang Monkey is a Primary-class tower that was released in the initial release of Bloons TD 6. The tower retains its role from the Bloons TD  4, 5, and Classic game generations, but has changed its name from "Boomerang Thrower" along with other significant changes.  Like most Primary Monkeys, the Boomerang Monkey was featured in a teaser prior to the game's release.
Boomerang Monkeys throw boomerangs that pop 4 bloons each and throws them every 1.2s. The monkey has a base range of 43. Boomerangs move in a curved fashion in a counter-clockwise direction from the monkey's right hand. Boomerang Monkeys also feature a Change Hands option to target priority that swaps hands, reversing the origin and direction of thrown boomerangs.
There are various upgrades that greatly improve the Boomerang Monkey's popping power, including ricocheting glaives, cybernetic enhancements for rapid throwing, and heavy straight-thrown boomerangs that can re-hit bloons.
It costs $270 on Easy, $315 on Medium, $340 on Hard, and $380 on Impoppable if Cheap 'Rangs Monkey Knowledge is not active. Otherwise, if Cheap 'Rangs is active, the Boomerang Monkey costs $220, $265, $290, and $330 respectively.

Description[]
Similarly to its BTD5 counterpart, the Boomerang Monkey wears a jumpsuit. Its pattern is somewhat different, but more or less the same. Its official portrait depicts it throwing a boomerang with its dominant hand. The official portrait swaps direction if using Change Hands. When idle, the Boomerang Monkey may inspect its boomerang.
Path 1 generally contains upgrades leading towards the throwing of glaives, with each upgrade increasing the power of the boomerangs themselves into bouncing deadly glaives. Glaive Lord now surrounds itself with deadly glaives alongside throwable shredding glaives that pulverize blimps.
Path 2 generally deals with speed, providing the Boomerang Monkey with faster attacks on bloons. It also deals specialized damage bonuses to MOAB-class starting from Bionic Boomerang and above, and Turbo Charge provides turbotastic attack speed and increased base damage via its ability, topping up with Perma Charge with permanently attacks super quickly and does even more base damage while converting its ability into a great damage boost.
Path 3 generally deals with the element of throwing tough Kylie boomerangs that are thrown further, leading from simply throwing further to hot boomerangs that pop bloons of any type to actual Kylie boomerangs that can be upgraded to deal even more damaging effects to the bloons, particularly MOAB-class on higher upgrades.
As with all towers in BTD6, only two paths may be selected for a tower at one time, and only one of the selected paths may be upgraded to third-tier and beyond.

Target Priorities[]
The base Boomerang Monkey uses the standard target priorities: First, Last, Close, and Strong. All of its attacks are dependent on its given target priority.

First: Targets the first bloon visible within its range.
Last: Targets the last bloon visible within its range.
Close: Targets the closest bloon visible within its range.
Strong: Targets the strongest bloon visible within its range.
Certain alternate attacks have a different set of target priorities:

Glaive Lord's orbital glaives are always set on Close by default, for the sake of pierce-consumption calculations.
MOAB Press's anti-MOAB kylies target any MOAB-class bloon except BADs, and are based on main targeting.
MOAB Domination's anti-MOAB kylies target any MOAB-class bloon, and are based on main targeting.
Glaive Dominus's alternate attacks are based on those for Glaive Lord and MOAB Domination, respectively.
Boomerang Monkey has a unique target option "Change Hands", allowing the Boomerang to throw boomerangs from opposite angles in the opposite direction. This special target option is a set of alternating target priority buttons on the upgrade HUD; tapping on the button will swap position of the active button aligned with the corresponding Boomerang Monkey's dominant arm. The "Change Hands" target option was formerly limited to the "Ambidextrous 'Rangs" MK, but the target option had been fully introduced into the main Boomerang Monkey since Version 21.0.

Strategy[]
See also: Boomerang Monkey (BTD6)/Strategies
Summary[]
The Boomerang Monkey throws boomerangs that move in a curve and generally have a moderate amount of pierce. It can utilize its curved trajectories to hit bloons along the way, and has a special Change Hands targeting priority to alter the direction of the boomerangs. The Boomerang Monkey generally has better utility than the Dart Monkey, with many upgrades that are stronger against grouped rounds and multiple upgrades that specifically excel against MOAB-class bloons and Ceramics, but it has a glaring weakness to Camo Bloons.

Tips[]
In CHIMPS, 0-1-1 Boomerang can be afforded to reach a wide amount of track, especially in multi-lane maps.
Glaive Lord and Glaive Dominus has a good synergy with The Final Harmonic since it can trigger the damage-over-time effects they apply.

Boomerang Monkey Plush has officially been teased on June 18th 2023 on Ninja Kiwi's Twitter account, posting the teaser for Sauda Plush, Boomerang Monkey Plush, and BFB Jumbo Plush. A Ninja Kiwi Twitter giveaway was posted on June 20th 2023, with the end date on June 21st at 2pm (ET). On June 23rd 2023, the official announcement for these three plushes were announced on this post. Final week announcement for these plushes was posted on July 7th 2023.
The Boomerang Monkey's campaign can be seen on their entry on the Makeship website. Boomerang Monkey Plush costed $29.99 USD.
Boomerang Monkey Plush campaign ran from June 23rd 2023 to July 15th 2023 (specifically 11:00am NZST). At the end of the campaign, 1734 Boomerang Monkey Plushes were sold, funding 867% of the campaign. They were manufactured starting from July 14th 2023, and they were shipped by October 7th 2023.

Trivia[]
Boomerang Monkey's third path gives it an Australian look, seeing as boomerangs were used by Aboriginal people.
The Glaive Dominus Upgrade refers to the boomerang tower in the first person with the use of "my". This is the only tower and only upgrade in the game besides heroes that refers to itself in the first person.
The word Glaive usually refers to a polearm, not a kind of throwing weapon. The use of the word in Bloons is a reference to Science Fiction film Krull, where a weapon like the first upgrade path uses is called a Glaive.
The x-3-x upgrade replaces 1 arm with a bionic one, but it suddenly becomes a normal arm and vice versa when you swap hands.
