Strategy
See also: Engineer Monkey (BTD6)/Strategies
Summary

Engineer Monkeys contain various lower-tier and higher-tier upgrades that are specifically geared to support the defense, typically offering strong indirect roles for the defense via their various sentries and other contraptions. An Engineer Monkey is typically best paired with additional defenses to make use of their supporting roles, but also possess many powerful upgrades that focus on DPS as well as support.

Tips
Engi + Dart is a solid early-game on CHIMPS, with Engi doing most of the group-popping and Dart offering a little extra popping of stray bloons.

 Overclock ability now has an initial cooldown of 15 seconds before usage when the upgrade is first purchased.
 Overclock ability can now target itself.
