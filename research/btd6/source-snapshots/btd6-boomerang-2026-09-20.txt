Strategy
See also: Boomerang Monkey (BTD6)/Strategies
Summary

The Boomerang Monkey throws boomerangs that move in a curve and generally have a moderate amount of pierce. It can utilize its curved trajectories to hit bloons along the way, and has a special Change Hands targeting priority to alter the direction of the boomerangs. The Boomerang Monkey generally has better utility than the Dart Monkey, with many upgrades that are stronger against grouped rounds and multiple upgrades that specifically excel against MOAB-class bloons and Ceramics, but it has a glaring weakness to Camo Bloons.

Tips
In CHIMPS, 0-1-1 Boomerang can be afforded to reach a wide amount of track, especially in multi-lane maps.
Glaive Lord and Glaive Dominus has a good synergy with The Final Harmonic since it can trigger the damage-over-time effects they apply.
