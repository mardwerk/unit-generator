# Bounded authoring evaluation

The two-stage design was documented for the historical v8 default. Current `planned-v1` separates character design from numerical authoring. It plans a sourced repertoire, signature, base attack and three purchasing reasons with T1 through T5 milestones. A second stage implements those decisions through typed mechanics. Retained plan exposes weaknesses, capstone value, crosspath contributions and deliberate omissions. Edited requirements and revisions use same process. This is neither an autonomous optimizer nor balance certification.

[Evidence extraction](../../src/core/blueprint/evidence.ts) selects at most 96 exact passages and 18,000 characters for planning. [Planning](../../src/core/blueprint/plan.ts) uses full worked Dart and Boomerang progressions, constrains citations to supplied IDs and retains source-period limits. Compiler resolves arithmetic and legal builds; semantic review compares result with plan and analytical T4/T5 spending alternatives. Each authoring stage permits one repair by default.

Former [fixed-recipe route](../../src/core/blueprint/reference-authoring.ts) remains an explicit benchmark. It maps a compatible attack to one complete arrangement and is unsuitable as a general character-design strategy: different characters can inherit identical mechanics. Its narrow eligibility still prevents it from overriding edited requirements. The [five recipes](REFERENCE-PATTERNS.md) remain useful executable references and regression fixtures.

[Compatibility and the catalogue](../../src/core/mechanics/reference-patterns.ts) protect assumed rules and scale. [Blueprint validation](../../src/core/mechanics/validate.ts), [build resolution](../../src/core/mechanics/resolve.ts) and [design-policy checks](../../src/core/mechanics/design-policy.ts) enforce legal purchases, inherited modifiers, supported operators and selected policy. Catalogue test covers all 320 legal selections. These checks cannot establish character fit, truthful effect names or fun.

The [evaluation runner](../../scripts/evaluate-unit-pipeline.mjs) retains requests, model responses, attempts, usage and reports. Freeze corpus, bounded evidence, Definition, recipe version, model settings and expected-fit rubric before a batch. Read completed reports before changing next experiment; do not tune against intermediate candidates. Compare source-supported attack behavior, faithful limitations and truthful names separately from structural success. Exact code-owned quotes prove source membership, not actor attribution, negation, story period or permission to omit a condition. Lexical filter can miss valid attacks and admit misleading context. A model review can flag issues, but its score cannot certify its own output. Actual user acceptance remains a separate decision.

Completed sequential development batch `.runs/unit-pipeline/final-benchmark-2026-09-21T10-30-38-932Z` produced all nine expected positive drafts on their first calls and blocked all six sparse-source cases before any model call. These are 15 development attempts, not 15 independent characters or a general fidelity guarantee. A subsequent enriched-source batch returned eight structural passes, but independent reading found two missing historical-skill qualifications. Final-code offline replay of all 17 positive selections made those limits visible and removed misleading reserves, without new model calls. That batch supported historical v7 starter promotion; current default is `planned-v1`. [Pipeline evaluation](../../docs/PIPELINE-EVALUATION.md) records separate live and replay evidence, Dots check, two final successful fresh-retrieval Rimuru CLI runs and earlier refusals. These reviewed cases are no longer untouched holdouts for resulting fixes.

Include both useful positive cases and honest refusals. A concrete energy beam should fit nonburn energy recipe even if character also has unsupported teleportation. A biography or unspecified ability copying should not become an invented attack. Shared character entries must not transfer another person's powers. Current contract excludes names-only skill inventories; a larger source is not automatically better if bounded retrieval retains mostly headings. Report positive acceptance, false refusal, invented fit, structural failure and provider failure separately; do not collapse them into one success percentage. Once a held-out case influences tuning, retire it from holdout and reserve new cases.

Historical optimizer proposal below remains unimplemented. Current next actions and study protocol live in [NEXT-EXPERIMENT](../../docs/NEXT-EXPERIMENT.md) and [REFINEMENT](../../docs/REFINEMENT.md). Keep this bounded proposal as design history, not execution authorization. Freeze one baseline and at most two candidate changes, each testing one stated hypothesis, on six development cases. Generate each case once for each variant, then repeat baseline and finalist twice on three untouched holdouts. This historical proposal allowed at most 30 initial generations and one normal repair per generation. Current two-stage default instead permits one repair per stage, up to 120 calls for 30 generations, excluding separately budgeted critique. Freeze actual stage and repair limits before execution. Record all attempts and failures. Set call, token, wall-time and provider-specific money ceilings before execution, including any independent critique calls. No paid fallback or extra retry may exceed caller's existing authorization. Stop when any ceiling is reached or three consecutive provider failures prevent useful evaluation, retaining partial results.

Compare only candidates that preserve hard gates and explicit user choices. Judge fidelity, distinct path uses, early complexity, ordinary output, active peak, availability and cost separately. A higher capacity proxy or a larger model self-score is not an improvement by itself. Require reviewed held-out gains before promoting a default; a small benchmark supports counts and concrete findings, not a claim of general superiority.

A future per-character search could try at most three coherent repertoire and branch plans against same frozen evidence, with one repair each and a separately budgeted critique. It should return surviving alternatives with tradeoffs or a precise evidence gap, never loosen hard gates to manufacture a winner. Search remains unimplemented. Add a recipe only for a recurring supported need that existing arrangements cannot meet, such as nonburn energy with control. Add an engine operator, such as an independent attacker, only when its targeting, ownership, inheritance and legal-build tests are defined. Extend demonstrated gap rather than rewrite whole generator.
