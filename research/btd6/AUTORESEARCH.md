# Bounded authoring evaluation

The v8 default separates character design from numerical authoring. It plans a sourced repertoire, signature, base attack and three purchasing reasons with T1 through T5 milestones. A second stage implements those decisions through the typed mechanics. The retained plan exposes weaknesses, capstone value, crosspath contributions and deliberate omissions. Edited requirements and revisions use the same process. This is not an autonomous optimizer or a balance certificate.

[Evidence extraction](../../src/core/blueprint/evidence.ts) selects at most 96 exact passages and 18,000 characters for planning. [Planning](../../src/core/blueprint/plan.ts) uses full worked Dart and Boomerang progressions, constrains citations to supplied IDs and retains source-period limits. The compiler resolves arithmetic and legal builds; semantic review compares the result with the plan and analytical T4/T5 spending alternatives. Each authoring stage permits one repair by default.

The former [fixed-recipe route](../../src/core/blueprint/reference-authoring.ts) remains an explicit benchmark. It maps a compatible attack to one complete arrangement and is unsuitable as a general character-design strategy: different characters can inherit identical mechanics. Its narrow eligibility still prevents it from overriding edited requirements. The [five recipes](REFERENCE-PATTERNS.md) remain useful executable references and regression fixtures.

[Compatibility and the catalogue](../../src/core/mechanics/reference-patterns.ts) protect the assumed rules and scale. [Blueprint validation](../../src/core/mechanics/validate.ts), [build resolution](../../src/core/mechanics/resolve.ts) and [design-policy checks](../../src/core/mechanics/design-policy.ts) enforce legal purchases, inherited modifiers, supported operators and the selected policy. The catalogue test covers all 320 legal selections. Those checks cannot decide whether the chosen attack belongs to the character, whether a name misrepresents an effect, or whether the resulting unit is fun.

The [evaluation runner](../../scripts/evaluate-unit-pipeline.mjs) retains requests, model responses, attempts, usage and reports. Freeze the corpus, bounded evidence, Definition, recipe version, model settings and expected-fit rubric before a batch. Read completed reports before changing the next experiment; do not tune against intermediate candidates. Compare source-supported attack behavior, faithful limitations and truthful names separately from structural success. Exact code-owned quotes prove source membership, not actor attribution, negation, story period or permission to omit a condition. The lexical filter can miss valid attacks and admit misleading context. A model review can flag issues, but its score cannot certify its own output. Actual user acceptance remains a separate decision.

The completed sequential development batch `.runs/unit-pipeline/final-benchmark-2026-09-21T10-30-38-932Z` produced all nine expected positive drafts on their first calls and blocked all six sparse-source cases before any model call. These are 15 development attempts, not 15 independent characters or a general fidelity guarantee. A subsequent enriched-source batch returned eight structural passes, but independent reading found two missing historical-skill qualifications. Final-code offline replay of all 17 positive selections made those limits visible and removed misleading reserves, without new model calls. The v7 starter default is now enabled; [pipeline evaluation](../../docs/PIPELINE-EVALUATION.md) records the separate live and replay evidence, Dots check, two final successful fresh-retrieval Rimuru CLI runs and the earlier refusals. These reviewed cases are no longer untouched holdouts for the resulting fixes.

Include both useful positive cases and honest refusals. A concrete energy beam should fit the nonburn energy recipe even if the character also has unsupported teleportation. A biography or unspecified ability copying should not become an invented attack. Shared character entries must not transfer another person's powers. The current contract excludes names-only skill inventories; a larger source is not automatically better if bounded retrieval retains mostly headings. Report positive acceptance, false refusal, invented fit, structural failure and provider failure separately; do not collapse them into one success percentage. Once a held-out case influences tuning, retire it from the holdout and reserve new cases.

The next optimizer should stay bounded. Freeze one baseline and at most two candidate changes, each testing one stated hypothesis, on six development cases. Generate each case once for each variant, then repeat the baseline and finalist twice on three untouched holdouts. This is at most 30 initial generations, with at most one normal repair per generation. Record all attempts and failures. Set call, token, wall-time and provider-specific money ceilings before execution, including any independent critique calls. No paid fallback or extra retry may exceed the caller's existing authorization. Stop when any ceiling is reached or three consecutive provider failures prevent useful evaluation, retaining partial results.

Compare only candidates that preserve hard gates and explicit user choices. Judge fidelity, distinct path uses, early complexity, ordinary output, active peak, availability and cost separately. A higher capacity proxy or a larger model self-score is not an improvement by itself. Require reviewed held-out gains before promoting a default; a small benchmark supports counts and concrete findings, not a claim of general superiority.

A future per-character search could try at most three coherent repertoire and branch plans against the same frozen evidence, with one repair each and a separately budgeted critique. It should return surviving alternatives with tradeoffs or a precise evidence gap, never loosen hard gates to manufacture a winner. This search is proposed, not implemented. Add a recipe only for a recurring supported need that existing arrangements cannot meet, such as nonburn energy with control. Add an engine operator, such as an independent attacker, only when its targeting, ownership, inheritance and legal-build tests are defined. Extend the demonstrated gap rather than rewrite the whole generator.
