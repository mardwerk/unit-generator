# Generated Luffy examples

These are actual outputs from the completed 2026-09-09 comparison, selected by reviewers before model identities were revealed. JSON formatting is normalized; candidate data is unchanged. Both selected outputs came from Astra Low integrated research and creation. They illustrate different contracts and briefs, so their numerical outputs are not a balance comparison.

The [approved manual design](../../../docs/references/luffy-unit-design-v0.1.md) remains the design reference. These generated outputs do not replace it or become gold executable references. The [evaluation](../../../docs/two-lane-evaluation-20260909.md) records all attempts, costs, review findings and limits.

## MangaMayhem with explicit direction

[luffy-manga-mayhem.generated.json](luffy-manga-mayhem.generated.json) is candidate `C-b920abbea2e7`, attempt `manga-mayhem-explicit-astra-low-integrated-r3`, from run `luffy-two-lane-20260909`. It passed after one repair. The reviewer selected it for complete executable coverage of the requested kit and clear adaptation limits.

Luffy punches with stretched limbs from a stationary position. Conqueror's, Observation and Armament each have five purchases. Any path unlocks shared Gears; one Technique action changes with the active form. Values below precede Haki modifiers and are provisional game values.

| Unlock          | Form             | Main attack                                 | Technique                                 |
| --------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Base            | Base             | 30 damage every 1.2 seconds, reach 10       | None                                      |
| Any path tier 1 | Second           | Fast contacts, 25 damage every 0.55 seconds | Five locked contacts                      |
| Any path tier 2 | Third            | 95 damage, up to five targets               | Larger impact, up to seven targets        |
| Any path tier 3 | Second + Third   | Faster giant fist, up to four targets       | Two locked giant contacts                 |
| Any path tier 4 | Fourth, Boundman | 145-damage concentrated punch               | 340-damage heavy contact                  |
| Any path tier 4 | Fourth, Snakeman | 48 damage every 0.45 seconds, reach 16      | Six locked contacts                       |
| Any path tier 4 | Fourth, Tankman  | Broad impact, up to six targets             | Repulse up to four distance units         |
| Any path tier 5 | Fifth            | Area contacts, maximum eight-second burst   | Displacement and 35% slow for 2.5 seconds |

Stamina capacity is 120, Gear entry requires 30, and Base restores 12 per encounter second. Techniques cost 24 and share a nine-second cooldown. Switching Gears requires passing through Base with a two-second reentry delay. Legal specialization uses at most two paths, with the secondary at tier 2 or below.

All requested forms execute in the retained probes. Fourth variant names and jobs remain provisional because the available source did not verify them individually. Terrain rubberization is bounded to displacement and slow; defensive Tankman, flight, evasion, water and Sea-Prism interactions do not execute. The combination Technique reduces sustained damage in sampled 12-second opportunities; its possible early-burst benefit needs testing. Several generated-candidate switching, retargeting and funding boundaries remain unobserved. No balance claim follows from stationary probes.

## BTD6-derived with direction withheld

[luffy-btd6-derived.generated.json](luffy-btd6-derived.generated.json) is candidate `C-66c10eba05a2`, attempt `btd6-derived-withheld-direction-astra-low-integrated-r1`, from the same run. It passed without repair. It did not receive the preferred Haki-path and shared-Gear direction. No explicit BTD6 candidate passed in this study.

The base uses radius-36 elastic contact attacks. Elastic Force increases ordinary reach and crowd contact; Haki adds detection, damage and activated control; Gear Evolution adds timed attack replacements. Gear Third belongs to Elastic Force, while Second, Fourth and Fifth belong to Gear Evolution. This is a tower adaptation with separate abilities and fixed attack snapshots, without MangaMayhem stamina or shared contextual Techniques.

The candidate has known defects retained for inspection:

- Conqueror's Presence promises up to 40 targets but stuns only one in executed dense probes.
- Transformations drop purchased Observation and other base attack modifiers, so they cannot hit concealed targets after buying detection.
- Gear Third adds distance-50 reach but lowers nearby output in the sampled window.
- Transformation durations cover the entire 12-second probe, so expiry, restoration and cooldown sequences remain unobserved in this candidate.
- Resource exhaustion, incoming rubber defenses, environmental awakening, flight, water and Sea-Prism restrictions do not execute.

The examples contain generated designs only. Captured BTD6 game files and character source pages remain in private local evidence storage.
