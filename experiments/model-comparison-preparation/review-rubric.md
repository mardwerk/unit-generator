# Content review rubric v0.1

Use this rubric before viewing model identity, reasoning, prompts, cost or latency. Reviewers may inspect the final candidate, selected lane contract, eligible source evidence, relevant execution observations and the explicit brief only when evaluating that track. Generator self-assessments are separate assertions to inspect after the initial content judgment. Cite artifact locations or observed behavior for each conclusion.

| Dimension                      | Required record                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Validity                       | Pass, fail or unchecked for each actual schema, reference, legal-build and runtime contract check. Name checks and coverage. Acceptance establishes only those checks.                                                                                                                                             |
| Required behavior              | For each explicit feature, record fulfilled, adapted, missing or unsupported. Fulfilled needs reachable behavior and supporting evidence. An acknowledged unsupported requirement remains unmet. In the withheld track assess only supplied instructions as requirements.                                          |
| Source fidelity                | For each material power or limitation, record source-supported, authored adaptation, unsupported claim or unresolved evidence. Cite the source passage and final behavior. Valid IDs do not establish entailment; missing evidence is not proof that a claim is false.                                             |
| Description agreement          | Record each material agreement or contradiction between player-facing promises and actual operations, timing, targets or lifecycle. Unexecuted intended behavior stays unchecked.                                                                                                                                  |
| Purchase and branch usefulness | Record observed useful, conditionally useful, observed unchanged, observed regressing or untested, with scenario and legal-build context. Treat detection, support, immunity, map coverage and numeric purchases according to their intended jobs. No observed benefit in one scenario is not a universal failure. |
| Lifecycle and crosspaths       | Record which activation, replacement, recovery, resource, upgrade and legal-crosspath behaviors were exercised, which failed and which remain untested. Use lane-specific probes when available.                                                                                                                   |
| Pairwise judgment              | For each same-lane, same-track pair, prefer candidate A, prefer B, tie or incomparable. Give the concrete reason and any dimension that favors the other candidate. Incompatible supported scopes or missing evidence may justify incomparable.                                                                    |

The source-fidelity judgment considers evidenced defining traits and limitations even when the intended design direction is withheld. Matching the private Luffy progression is an exploratory observation in that track, not an instruction-following requirement. Both tracks remain development evidence.

After content judgments are recorded, reveal operational evidence. Report all attempts, accepted candidates, research failures, transport failures, repairs, known and unknown usage, reserved allowance and end-to-end/stage latency separately by lane and track. Never count an unrun budget-limited slot as a successful or failed generated candidate. Retain it in the planned matrix.

There is no weighted total or automatic winner. Report tradeoffs and disagreement. A unit may be valid yet unfaithful, useful in one scenario yet weak in another, or preferred for its design while more expensive to generate. Gameplay balance remains unassessed without a declared game context and sufficient evaluation.
