# Nami, Art of Weather

Stationary Straw Hat weather artillery, One Piece manga through the end of Wano. Source-backed weather science supplies lightning, Gust Sword, Heat Egg and Raitei. Zeus already resides inside her Sorcery Clima-Tact at placement, not in a standalone summoned body. Generous collision tolerance and purchasable prelaunch retargeting approximate his assistance; true homing, weapon reshaping and consciousness transfer are omitted. Heat Egg canonically forms a hot orb at the staff's end; its scheduled, launched aimed-shot delivery here is an authored limitation/adaptation, not an established projectile feat. Snow melting and terrain interactions are not simulated. Gust Sword displaces eligible displaceable targets without passing the path entrance. Lightning applies no movement slows, stuns or substitute hostile debuffs. All numbers, prices, schedules, unlocks and stamina costs are provisional game tuning, not canon measurements; prices await measured balance. Cadence upgrades abstract practiced weather setup, not speed magic. Stamina represents Technique preparation, not transformation exhaustion. The highest purchased tier on any path unlocks Gust Sword at 1 and replaces it with Raitei at 5 on the single shared Technique button. Purchases are cumulative under the maximum 5-2-0 rule. Enemy movement is caller-supplied. Mirage Tempo, Fata Morgana, Milky Road travel/terrain, showers, Impact Dial absorption/recoil, navigation, theft, negotiation and financial management are omitted. No healing, ally damage buffs, regeneration denial, elemental-immunity bypass or later-arc feats.

Continuity: One Piece manga through the end of Wano Country arc.
