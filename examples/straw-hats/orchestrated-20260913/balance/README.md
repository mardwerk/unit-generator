The price changes are generated artifacts. Their reports use `derived-price-only`: exact non-price executable equality was checked, then costs, purchase prices and affordable-build frontiers were recomputed from all measured builds. Original measurement hashes and reports remain linked. Shared highest-tier form unlocks are included in the complete build enumeration. Sanji was also checked at 250-credit increments from 1,000 through 5,000; cheaper cook-funded modes improve some intermediate results but do not lead the tested combat frontiers.

Chopper healed 70 HP at base cost 48 in this explicit demand schedule. Several cheap copies could therefore be an economy problem if the host permits them to heal shared recipients freely. These single-placement runs neither establish additive healing nor test overheal, stacking, placement caps or shared budgets. Sanji and Chopper support comparisons are conditional on a host that supplies living injured allies; no tower HP or incoming enemy attacks were invented.

Movement uses 0.25-second caller steps and runtime speed modifiers; displacement is preserved. Bosses and control-immune waves explicitly reject weak-will, stun, slow and displacement eligibility. No obstacle probe produced contact for this roster. Short-lived boundary timing and untested geometry remain limits.

Hearing protocol versions are preserved in [protocols.json](protocols.json). [tag-audit.json](tag-audit.json) checks every final unit; only Brook depends on `can-hear`.
