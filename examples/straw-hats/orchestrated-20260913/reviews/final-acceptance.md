Final receipts retain `manualUnitEdits: false`, source-fidelity findings, qualification results and normalized output hashes. The final comparison retains the original measurement hash and the final unit hash for every cost derivation. Only explicitly allowed price fields and separately reviewed description differences can differ. All other fields must match before costs and affordability are recomputed. The new preservation hook additionally prevents unrequested generator changes from passing the standard validation/repair flow.

The four price decisions preserve measured specialist roles: visible-wave artillery, ordinary and concealed control, armored-target pressure, ranged damage, secondary cooking support and cheap medical support. [Balance evidence](../balance/README.md) records 18,487 moving probes, all 640 legal unit/build combinations and a measured benefit for all 150 purchases. Hearing eligibility, displacement distance and supplied-injury healing are assessed explicitly rather than hidden inside one damage score.
