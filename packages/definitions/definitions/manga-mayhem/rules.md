# MangaMayhem contract 0.1

Produce `mardwerk.manga-mayhem.unit`, version `0.1`. Names allow up to 256 characters; unit, path and purchase descriptions allow up to 2048 characters. Keep prose concise and mechanically accurate. This executable subset supports stationary units with direct-contact or traveling-projectile attacks. Direct contact can reach beyond ordinary melee range. Projectile delivery requires positive projectileSpeed and projectileRadius; single and area impacts are supported. Target obstacles, concealment and invulnerability remain separate eligibility checks. Periodic ally healing is supported. The optional mechanics block also supports scheduled actor attacks, periodic summons, projectile child graphs, income and range support through the common mechanical runtime. Defensive combat and automatic enemy path following are outside this version.

Use three cumulative five-purchase paths. Legal purchased tiers have at most two nonzero paths, with the smaller at most two. Every purchase declares a modifier. The highest purchased tier across any path unlocks forms and contextual Techniques. Base-only units are valid. Runtime forms, stamina and Techniques are optional capabilities, not requirements for a character adaptation.

There is one active primary profile. An alternate form replaces it, never adds a parallel attack. Stamina is required for positive-drain forms and all Techniques, including base-form Techniques. Zero-drain alternate forms are free, need no stamina and may switch directly between one another; use these for ordinary transformations without an exhaustion mechanic. It first becomes available at its declared tier, full; it drains in positive-drain forms and recovers in base or zero-drain alternate forms during encounter time. Purchases do not refill it. Entry requires the configured minimum. Changing between positive-drain forms requires a non-draining form and the reentry delay. A voluntary change waits for unresolved contacts, then carries remaining primary recovery fraction. Exhaustion cancels unresolved contacts, immediately returns to base and preserves the recovery deadline. An exactly funded final Technique contact resolves before exhaustion. Encounter reset clears attacks, counters and cooldowns and restores full stamina in base. Pausing means no advance call.

Each form may offer contextual Techniques by minimum tier. The highest unlocked one supplies the single Technique button. Pressing queues it for the next primary cycle; the request records the selected target, active form and Technique. Commit rechecks them, cooldown and funding, then charges stamina and starts the one shared cooldown. Funding includes continuous drain through the last hit and through any queued wait. A cancelled request spends nothing and resumes normal primary acquisition. The Technique replaces that primary cycle with its own windup plus recovery. Purchased speed modifiers affect primary timing only. Multi-hit Techniques lock their target; later hits miss if it dies or becomes ineligible. Techniques never advance primary counters.

Flat damage additions affect primary attacks and every Technique, including zero-base-damage utility Techniques: 0 plus a flat addition of 4 deals 4 before other modifiers. Describe those purchases as affecting all Techniques. Apply flat damage additions then multiply purchased damage multipliers, split the internal fraction, then apply remaining armor reduction only to ordinary damage. Internal damage bypasses armor, not invulnerability. Sum flat damage and reach. Multiply primary timing and damage factors. Detection and retargeting are enabled by any purchased modifier. Armor ignore, internal fraction and contact stun take the largest purchased value. Pulse, emission and support configurations each belong to at most one path, with later purchases replacing earlier configurations on that path. This bounded ownership rule prevents ambiguous crosspath replacement.

Area contact always includes its primary target in the cap. Other targets must be within primary reach and the impact area, visible to the unit, and unobstructed from the impact; select nearest the contact, breaking ties by ID. Sweeps require direct-contact delivery and single-hit Techniques; projectile sweeps and multi-hit sweep Techniques are invalid. Sweeps follow the line from the unit to the selected contact, without extending beyond it, and require unobstructed contact to each target. A successful primary cycle means at least one positive damage hit; it advances each counter once regardless of collateral. Retargeting replaces one lost primary target at resolution without restarting windup. It never retargets Techniques.

Contact and pulse stuns require both weakWilled and stunnable. Existing stun and its following protection interval block new stuns; protected targets do not consume pulse caps. At a simultaneous contact and pulse, apply the longer stun. For both pulse and emission, cycles is the number of successful primary contacts required to trigger one effect, not the number of waves produced. Raising cycles alone makes the effect less frequent. A pulse applies only stun and deals no damage; interval sets an additional minimum time between pulses. Pulse counters saturate while waiting for their minimum interval; emission has an independent primary counter and excludes the flat damage addition. Emission starts at contact and extends away from the unit, can exceed primary reach, stops at obstacles, and never triggers other effects or counters. Displacement requires displaceable and cannot pass the path entrance. Slow requires slowable; refresh duration without stacking magnitude. Neither creates terrain.

The deterministic encounter API accepts caller-supplied target motion at explicit encounter times. It reports control durations and displacement; it does not simulate enemy path movement or balance. Source fidelity, purchase value and enjoyable play require separate evidence. Do not silently encode unsupported behavior as damage.

Projectiles launch after windup at the then-selected target coordinate and arrive after distance/projectileSpeed seconds. The projectileRadius is the allowed distance of the locked target from that coordinate at impact; a target that moves outside it causes a miss. Area collateral is centered on that coordinate, respects visibility, line of sight and cap, and may exceed primary acquisition reach. Launching releases the primary cycle; multiple shots can travel at once. Launched damage modifiers are captured and neither form changes, exhaustion nor purchases cancel shots or change their travel time. A missed projectile has no collateral. Reset removes all shots. Primary retargeting can replace a lost target before launch, never after launch. These are aimed projectiles, not homing summons.

Optional unit.support and purchased modifiers.support have name, interval, radius, cap and heal. The first pulse occurs after one interval. Each pulse heals living injured allies within radius and unobstructed line of sight, lowest health fraction first, then ID, up to cap, never above maximum health. Allies and injury are supplied by the encounter caller. Support continues without enemies. Later support purchases replace the configuration and carry the fraction of the current interval; unlocking starts one full interval and reset restarts it. Support does not resurrect or grant damage buffs.

Optional onHit arrays on primary profiles and Techniques use the shared status schema. Status IDs define stacking groups. Stuns/slows, damage over time, damage taken modifiers and temporary or permanent property tags execute on the common clock. Set immunity/required tags, stacking scope and refresh policy explicitly. Manga canonical eligibility tags are weak-willed, stunnable, displaceable and slowable, matching the host booleans weakWilled, stunnable, displaceable and slowable. True booleans add their canonical tags; canonical tags infer omitted booleans. A false boolean with its tag is contradictory and rejected. Timed tag mutations update the same effective eligibility and restore it on expiry. Other tags require caller-supplied facts. requiresDamage controls whether a status needs positive applied damage. Damage over time first ticks after initialDelaySeconds plus intervalSeconds unless triggerImmediate is true. Damage taken additions and multipliers apply after outgoing damage/armor calculation. Original contactStun/pulse and Technique controls preserve their stricter Manga eligibility flags.

Optional unit.mechanics is the canonical mechanical model shared with Default. It declares attacks, actors, passiveSummons, income and rangeSupport arrays. All five arrays are required when the block exists; use empty arrays where unused. A scheduled attack has the shared ExecutableAttack fields plus intervalSeconds and projectiles. These attacks supplement the Manga primary and are not automatically suppressed by a Manga form change. Every declared actor requires a passiveSummons entry or a spawn-actor trigger, and actor IDs must resolve; timed actors expire on the same clock and launched projectiles continue independently. lifetimeSeconds zero means no timed expiry. Income runs only after caller startRound and uses bounded emissions, pickup lifetime and explicit collection policy. Range support uses unique stacking groups and explicit includesOwner; it changes ally effective range, never personal damage. This block is static across Manga purchases, so do not promise actor/income upgrades in descriptions that only modify personal damage.

A canonical scheduled projectile can use speed/radius for an aimed shot or a recursive projectile graph. Graphs expose straight, stationary and aimed-impact flight with contact/expiry/exhaustion/blocker child emission. Each child has its own collision radius, pierce and damage. Rich graph nodes hit individual colliders; use a stationary child with radius for a blast. Root attack onHit effects do not automatically apply to children. A projectile node may declare its own onHit statuses and targetFilter; these execute for that node, with missing target facts reported rather than guessed. appliesDamage false is a carrier without a damage operation; a true zero-damage hit may still receive target damage-taken additions. Do not invent omitted flight modes.

The encounter remove operation stops the Manga primary, pending unlaunched contacts, healing, new actor production and new income. Actor templates default to expireWithParent true; explicitly independent actors finish their authored lifetime. Launched projectiles and already-applied statuses continue. Reset restores the unit and restarts the model.

The same canonical mechanics block optionally declares zones, triggers, modifiers and accounts. Manga creates the shared encounter effects runtime and supplies damage/status hooks. Zones sample live enemies and apply declared damage and statuses. Triggers can execute named mechanical attacks or summon declared actors; round-start, round-end, target-spawn and target-pop facts come from the encounter, and other event facts may be dispatched explicitly by the caller. Ability-ID activation and timed apply-modifier trigger actions are not exposed in Manga. Mechanical modifiers affect scheduled attack damage, range, interval, detection and graph pierce, or income.amount; they do not change the Manga personal primary, Techniques, purchases or XP. Account operations use owner-local account IDs; endRound applies one common account round, and collected income enters the shared cash ledger. These optional capabilities do not change existing units that omit them.
