# Tower defense

Return a complete Unit using `schemaVersion: "btd6-derived/0.2"`. Describe the subject through the shared mechanics contract. Choose `resolution: "upgrades"` for an original generated unit. All three paths contain exactly five upgrades. A legal build uses at most two paths, with at most one path above tier two. Every purchase must change actual combat behavior in at least one immediate legal transition; cost or display range alone does not count.

The base and every resolved model contain targeting, attacks, abilities, actors, passiveSummons, income and support arrays. Empty arrays are valid for absent capabilities. A noncombat model can rely on income or support. Use exact endpoint overrides only for intentional combinations whose complete behavior differs from composed upgrades; overrides do not inherit into other builds. Null models are exclusively unresolved captured references and are invalid generated output.

Attack acquisition reach and projectile collision are separate. Global reach still declares obstacle handling. Projectile graphs own their speed, lifetime, collision radius, pierce and child emissions. Keep root graph damage and pierce consistent with their attack fields. A carrier without a damage effect sets `appliesDamage: false`; it can create damaging children on contact. Detection at acquisition and detection at collision may differ deliberately. Direct contact is appropriate for melee, including long-reach melee. Do not mislabel melee as a projectile merely because it reaches far.

Statuses declare their duration, immunity and stacking policy. Timed damage, control and target-property changes execute through the shared effect ledger. Actor templates have their own attacks; abilities can create timed actors and explicitly suppress parent attacks. Permanent actors and temporary transformations are separate concepts. Income produces per-round pickups with collection and expiry. Support declares recipients and stacking. Conditions and event reactions must reference available actions and required target facts.

Character mechanics that the contract cannot execute belong in `unsupported`. Deliberate gameplay changes belong in `adaptations`. Duration and cooldown do not implement a stamina resource. A static captured formula does not establish live game parity. Paragon fusion and hero progression use their separate policies; they are not temporary transformations.
